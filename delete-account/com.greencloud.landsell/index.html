Delete your KatastarPro account

Package: com.greencloud.landsell

In the app

  1. Open KatastarPro and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

By email

Send a request from your registered email to greencloud991@gmail.com. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • account (email / credentials)
  • optional profile fields (name, phone, address)
  • favorites and search history
  • free-search counters and subscription status stored by the App

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel Google Play billing. Cancel the subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
