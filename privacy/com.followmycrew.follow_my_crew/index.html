Privacy Policy - Follow My Crew

Last updated: 2026-10-04 · Package: com.followmycrew.follow_my_crew

Overview

Follow My Crew (“the App”) helps friends share a temporary live map for a private crew session (nights out, festivals, trips). Location sharing is intentional, session-based, and can expire automatically.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Approximate/precise location while you enable live sharing for an active crew.
  • Photos/captions you choose to upload as Moments (Firebase Storage).
  • Event details you create (title, time, location pin).
  • Basic device/app diagnostics needed to operate Firebase and maps.

How we use data

We use this data only to run core features: authentication, private crew maps, events, moments, and optional ads. We do not sell your location data.

Sharing within a crew

When sharing is on, your live position is visible to members of your active private crew. You can stop sharing or leave the crew at any time. Crew sessions may expire.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage)
  • Google Maps Platform
  • Google AdMob (advertising)

Those providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Retention & security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. You may request account/data deletion by contacting support. Inactive location session fields expire with the crew sharing window.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: support@followmycrew.app

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
